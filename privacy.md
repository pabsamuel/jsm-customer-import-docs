# Privacy Policy

**App:** Customer & Organization Import for Jira Service Management
**Publisher:** Atesen Software (independent developer, Türkiye)
**Contact:** sametatesen2@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 26 September 2026

## 1. What this app does with data

The app runs inside your Atlassian Cloud site on the Atlassian Forge platform. When a Jira administrator runs an import, the app processes:

- the contents of the CSV file the administrator uploads: email addresses, display names and organization names;
- the Atlassian account IDs, organization IDs and service space (project) IDs returned by Jira Service Management;
- the administrator's Atlassian account ID, so the app can show who started each job.

The app does not read your issues, comments, attachments or customer conversations.

## 2. Where data is stored

All processing and storage happen on Atlassian's Forge platform, inside Atlassian's cloud infrastructure. **The app has no servers of its own and sends no data to Atesen Software or to any third party.** There are no analytics services, no advertising networks and no sub-processors.

## 3. How long data is kept

Import rows and results are stored so you can watch progress, resume a paused job and download the result file. They are deleted automatically **30 days** after the job was created. You can delete a job and its data earlier from the Job history screen. Uninstalling the app removes the app's storage according to Atlassian's Forge data retention policies.

## 4. Logs

The app writes application logs for troubleshooting. These logs contain job IDs, counts, HTTP status codes and error codes. **They never contain email addresses, names or organization names.** Forge logs are stored by Atlassian and are visible to the publisher only through Atlassian's developer tooling, with Atlassian's own retention limits.

## 5. Roles

For the customer data processed by this app, your organization is the controller. Atesen Software acts as a processor to the limited extent the app runs on your site. Atlassian is the infrastructure provider; see Atlassian's own privacy policy for how they process data in your cloud site.

## 6. Your requests

To ask about data handled by this app, or to request deletion of app data on your site, write to sametatesen2@gmail.com. Data deletion for your own Jira Service Management customers is done in Atlassian Administration, not in this app.

## 7. Security

The app requests the minimum Atlassian scopes it needs to create customers and organizations and to add them to organizations and service spaces. It makes no outbound network calls. Access to the app's admin screen is limited to users with Jira administrator permissions.

## 8. Changes

Material changes to this policy are announced in the app's release notes on the Atlassian Marketplace listing.
