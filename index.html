Customer & Organization Import for Jira Service Management

A Forge app by Atesen Software. One CSV creates your JSM customers and organizations, links them, and gives you a result for every row.

  • Creates customer accounts and the organizations that don't exist yet
  • Adds customers to their organizations and to the service spaces you choose
  • Dry-run preview with a downloadable error file, and a result file for every run
  • Safe to re-run: existing customers are detected and skipped
  • Runs entirely on Atlassian — no external servers
